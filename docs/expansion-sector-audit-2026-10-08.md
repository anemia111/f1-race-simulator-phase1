# Extra-category sector audit — 2026-10-08

Scope: all 30 distinct circuits used by KYOJO, SUPER GT, WEC and INDYCAR.
28 have a source-backed timing configuration; WEC Barcelona and Monza remain
unavailable pending the 2026 event timing maps. This is a presentation/geometry
registration correction, not measured sector timing generated by the race engine.
The extra-category timing rows continue to show unavailable sector times.

## Accuracy and behavior

- Published distances are measured from the finish/control line, not the offset
  standing-start line. Fuji's start offset is 307m and must not become sector zero.
- Public course geometry and map-to-model control-line anchors are **derived**.
  They are not surveyed transponder coordinates. Diagram alignment has no certified
  spatial error bound. Suzuka uses the operator's historical domestic four-sector
  diagram, not the three-sector F1 map. WEC 2026 distances override F1 distances on
  shared circuits. The native F1/SF track definitions are not modified.
- Extra-course drawing spans 48 model units, matching the native course generator,
  so a shared car sprite no longer shrinks due to the former 180-unit course span.
- Source geometry is rotated to the registered control line; reverse OSM chains
  are corrected for Long Beach, Indianapolis oval, Nashville, Markham, Milwaukee
  and Laguna Seca. Underlying source vertices/official lap lengths are retained.
- Imported/ongoing saves preserve their state. Their old control-line offset is
  registered at display time. Opposite-direction legacy geometry gets unavailable
  timing instead of falsely labeled sectors; selecting an event again creates the
  corrected geometry. Existing custom geometry is not silently replaced.
- No equal-thirds fallback is drawn. Unavailable locations are disclosed in DATA
  and the existing sources panel, while the neutral track trace stays visible.
- INDYCAR road/street races use the three published broadcast timing spans.
  Ovals use named `SF > T1`, `T1 > T2`, `T2 > T3`, `T3 > T4`, `T4 > SF`
  timing-loop spans, not invented three-sector boundaries.

### Source inconsistencies retained explicitly

- Mid-Ohio's report repeats `0.966856mi` for `I10 to SF`, which would produce
  `2.731060mi` against a `2.258mi` lap. The final displayed span is derived from
  lap length minus the first two measured spans (`0.493796mi`); the inconsistent
  final value is not ingested as fact.
- Long Beach calls the middle broadcast span `I3 to I6` although the first ends
  at `I5`. The displayed generic sector names avoid asserting that mislabeled
  endpoints form a connected chain. Distances retain the published reference.
- Reported lap distance and the sum of measured section lengths can differ
  slightly (e.g. Barber's rounded advertised `2.3mi`). Drawing uses the sum of
  reference spans; the race engine keeps its independently published lap length.

Validation: all 30 courses are covered by the timing audit test, including explicit
unavailable cases; three/four/five-section wraparound drawing sums to one lap;
all extra-category races, persistence, FREE, telemetry and energy tests are run
before the normal desktop playtest and publication.
